Web search (optional)

Lead search uses free public search engines, which sometimes throttle automated searches. For dependable web results, run your own free SearXNG and enter its address (it needs the json format enabled). Leave empty to use DuckDuckGo only. OpenStreetMap and “paste websites” always work.

Geoapify places (optional, free plan)

Adds a second search of public business listings, using OpenStreetMap-based place data. Get a free key at myprojects.geoapify.com (no card). The free plan includes 3,000 credits/day and allows commercial use with attribution; Geoapify's docs also allow storing the results. A typical search uses about 30 credits: 1 to find the area, 1 per 20 listing results, and 1 per business detail. Keep the attribution shown under Find leads: © OpenStreetMap contributors and Powered by Geoapify. Leave empty to keep Geoapify off.
