# Explee prospecting — the operator's guide

**Who this is for:** anyone who runs the Code Tech Revenue Desk and uses **Find leads → Global prospect search**.
No technical knowledge is assumed. You do not need to touch the code to follow this guide.

**What Explee is used for here:** finding people and companies to sell Event Owner App to.
**What it is *not* used for:** sending email, WhatsApp, DMs or campaigns. This integration never
starts Explee outreach. The six Explee campaigns stay in `review`, autopilot is off, and the
project daily budget is `$0`. You write and send every message yourself from the CRM.

**The promise in one sentence:** Explee gets **$30 (3,000 credits) for the entire life of this
install**, paced to last **five years** (60 months), and searching for leads is free.

---

## 1. The three rules

1. **Searching is free. Contact lookups cost credits.**
   Every search stays inside Explee's free-results zone. Only the two enrichment buttons in a
   lead — work email and phone number — spend money, and only when a contact is actually found.
2. **The lifetime ceiling cannot be crossed.**
   The app counts every credit it spends against 3,000. When that is gone, paid lookups stop.
   Free searching and saving still work.
3. **This desk does not send outreach.**
   Explee supplies data. You decide who to contact, what to say, and when to stop.

---

## 2. Where it lives

Open the dashboard → **Find leads** tab. The gold-and-green **Global prospect search** panel is
Explee. The older free sources (web search, OpenStreetMap, Geoapify, paste-a-website-list) are
still underneath it and cost nothing.

A lead found through Explee lands in the normal **Leads** database with the tag `explee`. You
work it exactly like any other lead: stage, owner, tasks, notes, messages.

---

## 3. Your first search, step by step

1. **Choose People or Companies.**
   - *People* finds decision-makers at matching companies. Use this when you want a named human.
   - *Companies* finds the businesses themselves. Use this when you want the organisation first.
2. **Describe the companies.** Plain words are best:
   - `event planning companies`
   - `wedding venues`
   - `conference centres`
   - `event planners in Austin`
   Do not write a sentence. A short description returns better matches.
3. **Add job titles** (People mode only): `Founder, Owner, Events Manager, Marketing Director`.
   Separate them with commas. Two or three titles is usually enough; ten narrows the search too much.
4. **Choose countries** — or leave the box empty for **worldwide**. See §5.
5. **Pick how many new leads** you want: 10 to 100. The app only keeps people you have not seen before.
6. **Optionally tag an offer** so the new leads are linked to one of your products.
7. Click **Preview free matches**. Nothing is saved yet.
8. Read the preview. Uncheck anyone irrelevant. The summary line tells you how many were fetched,
   how many are new, and how many were already known.
9. Click **Save N to CRM**. The selected people/companies are added and remembered.

> **Preview is genuinely free.** It uses the free 10-result page of each request. No credits are
> charged and no lead is written until you press Save.

## 4. People mode vs Companies mode

| | People | Companies |
|---|---|---|
| Best for | Reaching a named decision-maker | Building a target-company list |
| Needs job titles | Yes | No |
| Lead name | Person's name | Company name |
| Company domain | Filled in (used later for email lookup) | Filled in |
| LinkedIn | Person's profile | Company page |
| Email lookup later | Yes, if first + last name + domain exist | Only if the company has a person's name on the lead |

Two colleagues at the same company stay **two separate leads** in People mode. That is deliberate.
The app does not merge them into one company record.

## 5. Countries and reaching outside Nigeria

**Leave Countries blank to search worldwide.** That is the default and it is the broadest option.

You can also type ISO-2 country codes, separated by commas:

| Place | Codes |
|---|---|
| Nigeria | `NG` |
| Ghana | `GH` |
| Kenya | `KE` |
| South Africa | `ZA` |
| United Kingdom | `GB` |
| Ireland | `IE` |
| United States | `US` |
| Canada | `CA` |
| United Arab Emirates | `AE` |
| Saudi Arabia | `SA` |
| Germany / France / Netherlands | `DE, FR, NL` |
| Singapore / Malaysia / Philippines | `SG, MY, PH` |

The **Quick regions** buttons fill the box for you:

- **Worldwide** — no filter.
- **Africa** — NG, GH, KE, ZA.
- **Europe** — GB, IE, DE, FR, NL.
- **North America** — US, CA.
- **Gulf** — AE, SA, QA.
- **Southeast Asia** — SG, MY, PH.

You can mix and match: `US, GB, AE, KE` is valid. Up to 20 codes are accepted.
A narrower country list is not cheaper — searching is free either way — but it makes the results
more relevant and reduces duplicates.

> **Tip:** if a worldwide search returns too many people from one country, add the countries you
> actually want. If it returns too few, clear the box and try again.

## 6. What things cost

1 credit = **$0.01**. The account starts with **3,000 credits = $30.00**.

| Action | Cost | Charged when? |
|---|---|---|
| Search people or companies (up to 10 results per request) | **0 credits** | Never, inside the free zone |
| Natural-language filter helper | **0 credits** | Never |
| Basic work-email lookup | **1.5 credits** (1.5¢) | Only if an email is found |
| Premium work-email lookup | **5 credits** (5¢) | Only if an email is found |
| Basic phone lookup | **15 credits** (15¢) | Only if a phone is found |
| Premium phone lookup | **30 credits** (30¢) | Only if a phone is found |

**A miss is free.** If Explee cannot find the email or phone, the balance does not move.

At the full $30, the theoretical maximum is roughly:

- **2,000** basic emails, or
- **200** basic phones, or
- **600** premium emails, or
- **100** premium phones, or
- any mix of the above.

In practice you will not spend it all: the five-year pacing (§7) keeps the monthly spend small.

## 7. Why $30 has to last five years — and how pacing works

The budget is **3,000 credits over 60 months**, which is about **50 credits per month**
(roughly 1.6 credits per day).

- **50 credits are available immediately** when you make your first paid lookup, so you can test
  the feature without waiting a month.
- After that, the remaining allowance unlocks **linearly**. At the end of month 1 you have earned
  another ~50 credits; at the end of year 1, about 600; and so on.
- **Unused allowance carries forward.** If you spend nothing for three months, you can spend the
  saved amount later — for example on a batch of phone numbers for your best-fit prospects.
- **The lifetime ceiling is hard.** Even if you turn pacing off, total spend can never pass 3,000.
- **The balance floor is 100 credits.** The app refuses a paid call that would take the Explee
  account below that. Keeping a cushion also keeps free searching alive, because Explee requires
  a positive balance even for free results.

### What 50 credits a month looks like

| Month plan | Credits | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| Email-first month | 50 | ~33 basic work emails |
| Balanced month | 50 | ~5 basic emails + 3 basic phones, or ~28 emails + 1 phone |
| Phone-heavy month | 50 | ~3 basic phones + 3 emails |
| Save for two months | 100 | ~6 basic phones, or ~66 emails |

Use email first. It is ten times cheaper than a phone number and easier to personalise at scale.
Spend phone credits only on leads that are clearly worth a call.

## 8. Saving leads and avoiding repeats

- **Preview does not save.** Only **Save N to CRM** writes leads.
- **Duplicates are filtered locally**, in three places:
  1. against every lead already in this CRM (domain, email, LinkedIn, name + company),
  2. against duplicates inside the same batch,
  3. against a persistent memory of everything Explee has already returned.
- **Deleting a lead does not make it reappear.** The persistent memory still remembers it.
- **The app never uses Explee's own exclude lists.** Those lists switch the free-results zone
  off, which would make every search paid. Local dedupe keeps searching free.

If a search says “no new people matched”, it usually means everything Explee returned is already
known. Change the definition, the job titles, or the countries — do not keep re-running the same
search.

## 9. Getting a work email or phone number

Open a lead from **Leads** (or from the Explee preview after saving). In the drawer, the
**Explee enrichment** card sits under the contact lines.

### Work email

- Needs: **first name + last name + company domain** (or a website the app can turn into a domain).
- Basic cost: **1.5 credits**. Premium cost: **5 credits**.
- The result is saved to the lead's email list and the card updates.
- If no email is found, you are told and **nothing is charged**.

### Phone number

- Needs: a **LinkedIn profile** on the lead.
- Basic cost: **15 credits**. Premium cost: **30 credits**.
- The result is saved to the lead's phone list in international format (e.g. `+14255551234`).
- If no phone is found, **nothing is charged**.

### Basic or premium?

Leave **Premium** unticked for normal work. Tick it only when:

- the person is a high-value target and the basic lookup missed, or
- the company has an unusual name/domain or the person has an international profile.

Premium is 3.3× the price for email and 2× for phone. It is not the default for a reason.

> **Never bulk-enrich.** The buttons are one lead at a time on purpose. Bulk enrichment is how a
> five-year budget disappears in an afternoon.

## 10. Reading the status strip

The top of the Explee panel shows four numbers and a bar:

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Available now** | Credits the five-year pace has unlocked and you have not spent yet |
| **Explee balance** | What is actually left on the Explee account, in credits and dollars |
| **Lifetime used** | Credits spent against the 3,000 lifetime ceiling |
| **Free searches today** | Free-zone requests used today, out of 50 |
| **Lifetime budget runway** | Percentage of the $30 used, and the bar |

The footnote under the bar shows the monthly pace, the remaining runway in months, and any
warning (for example a balance the app could not read).

A healthy pattern is a mostly empty bar, a balance close to 3,000, and a free-search count that
resets each day.

## 11. Troubleshooting

| What you see | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| “The daily free-search allowance is used up” | 50 free requests used today | Wait until tomorrow. Save credits; do not switch to paid search. |
| “Too many searches in one minute” | More than 10 requests in 60 seconds | Wait a minute and retry. |
| “The five-year pace has not unlocked that much yet” | The lookup costs more than the allowance earned so far | Wait for the next unlock, or choose email instead of phone. |
| “The lifetime ceiling has been reached” | All 3,000 credits are spent | Paid lookups are done. Free searching and saving still work. |
| “The account is at the safety floor” | The balance is at or near 100 credits | Top up, or keep the account read-only. |
| “Paid lookups are off” | The lifetime budget is set to 0 | Set a budget above 0 in **Settings → Explee**. |
| “Explee says the credit balance is too low” | Even free results need a positive balance | Top up the Explee account. |
| “No work email/phone found” | A genuine miss | Nothing was charged. Try premium on a high-value lead, or leave it. |
| “Needs a first and last name plus a company domain” | The lead is a company or a one-word name | Add a contact person, or use LinkedIn/website to find the name. |
| “Needs a LinkedIn profile” | Phone lookup has no profile to work from | Add the LinkedIn URL to the lead's socials first. |
| Status says “No API key” | `.env` has no `EXPLEE_API_KEY` | Add the key and restart the app. |
| Status says “Status unavailable” | The app could not reach Explee | Check the network/key, then press Refresh. |

## 12. House rules — what not to do

1. **Do not turn on Explee autopilot or start a campaign.** This desk sends its own messages.
2. **Do not add credits to the budget casually.** $30 is the agreement. Raising it spends money
   that is meant to last five years.
3. **Do not use Explee exclude lists in search.** They disable the free zone. Local dedupe already
   handles repeats.
4. **Do not ask for more than 10 results per request.** The app hard-caps this; a bigger page
   leaves the free zone and bills the whole page.
5. **Do not bulk-enrich.** One lead, one deliberate click.
6. **Do not contact someone who asked you to stop.** Set the lead to **Do not contact** immediately.
7. **Do not paste personal data you do not need.** Business contact details only.

## 13. Monthly routine (10 minutes)

1. Open **Find leads** and check the status strip.
2. Note the **lifetime used** number. It should be small and rising slowly.
3. Check the **Explee balance**. If it is near the 100-credit floor, top up or stop paid lookups.
4. Run two or three free searches in countries you actually want.
5. Save only the leads that fit Event Owner App.
6. Spend email credits on the best-fit new leads (about 1.5 credits each).
7. Spend phone credits only on leads you intend to call this month.
8. Work the leads in the CRM: stage, owner, task, first message.

Once a quarter, compare the **lifetime used** figure against the month number. At 60 months you
should be at or below 3,000. If you are far ahead, slow down — the pace guard will eventually
stop you, but it is better to plan than to hit the wall.

## 14. Data, privacy and fair use

- Explee returns **business contact data**. Use it for a genuine business reason.
- Identify yourself in every message and honour opt-outs immediately.
- Nigeria's NDPA applies to Nigerian contacts; GDPR applies to EU/UK contacts. Keep volumes
  proportionate and keep records of why you contacted someone.
- The app stores leads in `data/leads.json` and the Explee counters/memory in
  `data/explee-state.json`. Back up `data/` regularly (`npm run backup`).
- The API key lives in `.env` (or the server environment). Never commit `.env` or share it.

This is practical guidance, not legal advice.
